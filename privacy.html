Privacy Policy

KUK Clips Publisher · Last updated 23 November 2026

KUK Clips Publisher (“the app”, “we”) is a personal tool used by its owner to upload short video clips to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts and to read basic statistics about those videos. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner, for the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not create accounts for other people.

Information the app accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account, TikTok shares the following with the app, only after the owner approves it on TikTok's own sign-in screen:

  • Basic profile information: display name, avatar and account identifier.
  • Profile and statistics: follower, following, like and video counts.
  • A list of the account's own videos and their public details.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account.

When a YouTube account is connected, the app accesses the equivalent channel information and permission to upload videos through Google's YouTube API.

How the information is used

  • To upload and publish videos the owner has chosen to post.
  • To show the owner how their own videos are performing.

The information is not sold, rented, shared for advertising, or used to build profiles of anyone.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are held by the integration service Composio, which connects the app to TikTok and YouTube on the owner's behalf. Video files and statistics are kept only as long as needed to post a video or show its results. Access tokens are never published or shared.

Third-party services

The app relies on TikTok, YouTube (Google) and Composio. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • TikTok: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Composio: composio.dev/privacy

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time. On TikTok, open Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and websites, and remove the app. On Google, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Withdrawing access stops all further use of that account's data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
